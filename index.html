About Me

Crafting Visuals with Purpose & Impact

I’m Sunny Thakur, a passionate Video Editor with 3+ years of experience, turning raw footage & creative ideas into engaging visual content.

I specialize in Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, & Cinematic Content. My editing focuses on strong hooks, smooth pacing, creative transitions, motion graphics, sound design, & storytelling that keeps viewers engaged.

I’m a Video Editor for TEXWEB SOLUTION PVT. LTD. & INTERNSHIP CATALYST, creating impactful & engaging content that helps brands communicate their ideas & connect with their audience.

I believe good editing isn’t just about making a video look better — it’s about capturing attention, telling a story, & making people want to watch until the end.
